## 📈 SaaS Revenue & Retention Observatory
`MySQL` • `Python` • `Pandas` • `Excel` • `Power BI` • `DAX` 

**End-to-End Customer, Revenue & Subscription Analytics Platform**

A comprehensive analytics solution built using a real-world SaaS customer dataset to monitor revenue performance, customer retention, subscription behavior, and churn risk through executive dashboards and advanced SQL analytics.

#### Project Highlights

- Analyzed **7,043 customer records** across multiple business dimensions.
- Performed **30+ SQL analyses** including customer segmentation, cohort analysis, churn analytics, revenue intelligence, and window-function reporting.
- Developed **20+ DAX measures** including MRR, ARR, Churn Rate, Retention Rate, Revenue at Risk, Customer Lifetime Value, and Customer Share.
- Designed **5 executive Power BI dashboards** for revenue, retention, subscriptions, customer insights, and churn monitoring.
- Identified critical business insights including:
  - 56.4% of revenue originated from Month-to-Month customers.
  - Fiber customers generated 62.1% of total revenue.
  - Electronic Check users exhibited a 45.3% churn rate.
